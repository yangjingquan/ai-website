# 小肥羊开发工作室获客官网设计系统

## Context
Vue 3 marketing site for a solo full-stack developer serving merchants and growing businesses that need mini-programs, apps, CRM, ERP, and operational back offices. The redesign goal is higher trust and consultation conversion: concrete evidence, transparent boundaries, practical language, and a clear low-friction CTA.

## Visual direction
Keep the current teal/blue business-operations visual language. Use a calm, credible, structured B2B feel: pale blue-gray canvas, white cards, dark navy proof sections, teal primary action, blue secondary action, 8px corners, compact labels, dense but readable dashboards. Do not introduce new fonts, neon colors, decorative gradients, or generic stock photography.

## Conversion principles
- Replace unsupported decorative metrics with labeled proof or mark them as examples.
- Put a primary CTA for “免费需求评估 / 获取功能清单” in the hero and repeat it after proof sections.
- Add clear evidence cards: scenario, problem, delivered scope, timeframe, deliverables, and evidence status.
- State indicative budget bands and timing as “以评估为准”; make inclusions/exclusions and maintenance visible.
- Add security/operations notes: roles, logs, backups, payment callbacks, deployment ownership, and incident response.
- Use honest “示例案例 / 可按真实项目替换” labels wherever factual verification is not available.
